Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Who created Aura Player?

Aura Player was designed and developed from the ground up by Manikanta Amara. It was built for music lovers who want a fast, beautiful, and completely private streaming app without being forced into monthly subscription fees or bombarded with advertisements.

Why is Aura Player completely free with zero ads?

Aura Player was created as an open-source alternative to modern streaming bloatware. Instead of running expensive server backends that require advertising or subscription paywalls, Aura Player leverages on-device stream extractors directly on your phone. You get pure music with zero commercial middle-men.

Is this APK safe to install?

Yes, 100%. Aura Player does not ask for dangerous permissions (no contacts, no camera, no location, no SMS). The app runs purely on your device without storing your personal data or embedding any advertising trackers.

How is my privacy protected?

We do not collect analytics, we don't store your IP address, we don't require an email to sign up, and we have zero advertising trackers embedded. Your playback history and saved playlists are stored privately in your phone's local storage.

How do I update to newer versions?

Whenever a new update is released, simply download the new APK from this page and tap install. Android will update the app in place, preserving all your custom playlists and favorites.

What Android versions are supported?

Aura Player supports Android 8.0 (Oreo, API 26) all the way through Android 15. It runs smoothly on Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Nothing Phone, Motorola, and other Android devices.
