Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Who created Aura Player?

Aura Player was designed and developed from the ground up by Manikanta Amara. It was built for music lovers who want a fast, beautiful, and completely private media player with rich synchronized lyrics, retro skins, and seamless cross-platform sharing.

Why is Aura Player free and open source?

Aura Player was created as an open-source passion project celebrating music and lyrics. By performing playback extraction, metadata resolution, and lyrics synchronization directly on your device, there are no expensive corporate server clusters to monetize, keeping the experience completely non-commercial, private, and distraction-free.

Is this APK safe to install?

Yes, 100%. Aura Player does not ask for dangerous permissions (no contacts, no camera, no location, no SMS). The app runs purely on your device without storing your personal data or embedding any tracking software.

How is my privacy protected?

We do not collect analytics, we don't store your IP address, we don't require an email to sign up, and we have zero advertising trackers embedded. Your playback history, saved playlists, and custom synced lyrics are stored privately in your phone's local storage.

How do I update to newer versions?

Whenever a new update is released, simply download the new APK from this page and tap install. Android will update the app in place, preserving all your custom playlists and favorites.

What Android versions are supported?

Aura Player supports Android 8.0 (Oreo, API 26) and newer. It runs on Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Nothing Phone, Motorola, and other Android devices.
